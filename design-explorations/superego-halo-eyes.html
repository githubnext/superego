Variants 4
A

Centred

Optimizes for calm: two dots level in the middle of the head, looking straight at you. · Tradeoff interest: the face is neutral, so the tilt carries all the attitude.

Variant A · icon, sizes, Ego mark

What works

It’s the most legible face and reads like an emoji at a glance. The tilt plus a straight stare comes across as deadpan, which suits a conscience.

What risks

Straight-on eyes can feel watchful, and some will read the icon as "I'm judging you". That fits the name, if you're happy with it.

Pick this if

You want a quiet face and the halo to do the talking.

B

Glancing up

Optimizes for story: the eyes look up and right towards the raised side of the halo, as if it's just noticed it. · Tradeoff symmetry: the face is lopsided, so it's busier at mid sizes.

Variant B · icon, sizes, Ego mark

What works

It’s the most whimsical, with eyes and halo in one small gesture. The diagonal from the eyes to the halo's raised end ties the composition together.

What risks

At 32px the upward glance is a 1px nuance. It also implies the character is surprised, which may be more personality than you want.

Pick this if

You want the icon to have a tiny moment in it.

C

Side-eye

Optimizes for cheek: the eyes are shifted right, giving a sideways glance with the tilt. · Tradeoff warmth: side-eye reads as sly or suspicious, which is funny but less friendly.

Variant C · icon, sizes, Ego mark

What works

It’s the most distinctive silhouette at 64px, and the offset eyes and tilted halo make a strong asymmetric mark. It suits a conscience that keeps an eye on things.

What risks

Sly can tip into smug. The eyes crowd the right edge of the head, so it needs care in Icon Composer.

Pick this if

You like the conscience as a slightly suspicious watcher.

D

Wide & round

Optimizes for cuteness: bigger eyes set wider, closer to a mascot. · Tradeoff restraint: it's the least minimal, and edges towards a character or sticker rather than an app mark.

Variant D · icon, sizes, Ego mark

What works

Of the four, the eyes survive best at 32px. It’s the friendliest, and Ego with the same eyes becomes a lovable little blob.

What risks

Big eyes on a dot read as a cartoon blob (Kirby, a soot sprite), which sets a more playful tone for the whole product.

Pick this if

You want Ego to feel like a character people like.

How to pick a direction

Eyes disappear at 16px in all four, which is fine: the small size falls back to the plain tilted halo. Judge at 64px and up. The winner goes to Icon Composer as three layers: halo, head and eyes.
